About Many Maybes

A place for “what else?”

Many Maybes is an independent creative-experience project built around one simple idea: the same starting point can become many different things.

Why “Many Maybes”?

A maybe leaves room to explore. Many maybes make room for different people, interpretations and ways of making. The name is an invitation to keep looking beyond the first obvious answer.

Who it is for

Curious teenagers and adults, occasional artists, experienced makers and people who have not drawn in years. You can take part alone or explore alongside friends and family. Programming and artistic training are optional.

What is here today

Challenge 01 offers four fixed marks, a few clear constraints and space for your interpretation. You can download a printable sheet, work digitally, explore three illustrative gallery examples, and email your own work for review.

The examples are labelled as examples, not participant submissions. The project is at an early stage; additional challenges and community features will follow only when we can support them well.

Beyond drawing

The first challenge is visual, but the idea is broader than any one medium. We are interested in objects, patterns, words, mathematical starting points and different ways of thinking. Some challenges may include optional source code or transparent AI-assisted experiments.
