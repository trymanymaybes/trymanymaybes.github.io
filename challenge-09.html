Challenge 09 · Inference from absence

What’s missing?

Keep the three incomplete outlines and their gaps. Add surrounding clues that suggest something absent or hidden, without drawing the missing thing or completing the outlines.

Your challenge

  1. Keep the three incomplete outlines and their gaps. Add surrounding clues that suggest something absent or hidden, without drawing the missing thing or completing the outlines.
  2. Keep every original mark visible and preserve its position relative to the other marks. Do not erase, cover or move individual marks.
  3. You may rotate or uniformly resize the whole starter.
  4. Try three ideas before choosing one. Add at least two clues that make your interpretation understandable.

Rough sketches are welcome. You can also imagine the scene or describe your interpretation in words.
