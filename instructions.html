How to take part

Start small. See where it goes.

You need the starting image, a way to add to it and a little curiosity. No account, paid software or coding experience is required.

1. Choose a challenge

Browse all creative drawing challenges. Each page includes a starter image, its rules and a link to draw in the browser. Where available, download the printable PDF to work on paper. Downloaded starters work offline.

2. Keep the starter and follow its rules

Keep the original marks visible and their relative positions unchanged. You may rotate or uniformly resize the whole starter. Each challenge has its own additional constraint: for example, Challenge 02 asks you to leave the central gap blank. Read the rules on the challenge page before drawing.

Explore several interpretations and develop one. Rough sketches are welcome; there is no single correct answer.

3. Explore at your own pace

Change one assumption at a time: viewpoint, scale, time, material, function or what might be absent. Ask what details would make that interpretation believable. Compare your attempts and name what changed. You do not have to make something polished or completely unlike anything anyone has made before.

You can browse the gallery whenever you like. Trying first may help you discover your own direction, but viewing other interpretations never requires a submission.

4. Share only if you want to

Open Share yours, choose your challenge and sign in with Google. Select a JPEG or PNG up to 8 MB and 24 megapixels. Add a title, optional public pseudonym and explanation, then review the publication permission.

Prepare the image locally in your browser, check it for visible personal information, complete verification and choose Submit for review. Preparation alone does not upload your image. The prepared JPEG must be under 5 MB. Up to three submission attempts are allowed per account per UTC day across all challenges; failed attempts count.

Submitters must be 18 or older. A parent or guardian may submit a younger creator’s work with permission, without the child’s real name or contact details.

5. What happens after submitting?

Keep your submission receipt and use Check my submission on the submission page to see its status. Work is reviewed before publication; submitting does not guarantee a place in the gallery. Your email address is not used as a public credit.

Individual gallery pages provide a link and an image to share. Embed code is for websites and blogs; social apps generally use the page link or uploaded image instead. Comments are not open during this pilot.

Only share work you own or have permission to share. Tell us if you used code or AI assistance so we can describe the process accurately. See Privacy & participation for publication and removal details.

Common questions

What is Many Maybes?

Many Maybes is a collection of creative drawing challenges. Everyone starts with the same marks and uses a constraint to explore different interpretations.

Do I need drawing or coding experience?

No. Simple lines, rough sketches and ideas are welcome. Use paper or the freehand browser tools; turtle coding is optional.

Are the challenges free?

Yes. Browse the challenges, download available printables and draw in the playground for free.

Do I need an account?

You do not need an account to browse or draw. Google sign-in is required only for direct submissions for review.

How long should I spend?

There is no time limit. Try a few minutes for your first ideas, then continue for as long as you enjoy it.
