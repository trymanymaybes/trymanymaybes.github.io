How to take part

Start small. See where it goes.

You need the starting image, a way to add to it and a little curiosity. No account, paid software or coding experience is required.

1. Get your starting point

Download the printable Challenge 01 sheet, or use the PNG or SVG in a drawing tool you already know. Once downloaded, the activity works offline.

2. Follow the Challenge 01 rules

  • Keep all four original marks visible, in their original positions and orientation.
  • You may scale the whole starter uniformly. Do not move, erase or rotate individual marks.
  • Add lines, shapes, words or colour to turn the marks into a scene, an object, a story or another idea.
  • Give your interpretation a title. An explanation of your thinking is optional.

There is no single correct answer. These rules apply to Challenge 01; future challenges may use different constraints.

3. Explore at your own pace

If you feel stuck, write down a few things the marks remind you of. Try a second interpretation, or combine two ideas. You do not have to make something polished or completely unlike anything anyone has made before.

You can browse the gallery whenever you like. Trying first may help you discover your own direction, but viewing other interpretations never requires a submission.

4. Share only if you want to

Open Share yours and select a JPEG or PNG up to 8 MB and 24 megapixels. Add a title, an optional public pseudonym and an optional explanation, then review the publication permission.

The page prepares a resized JPEG locally in your browser, without the original file metadata. Check the picture itself for faces, signatures, addresses or other personal details.

  1. Download the prepared image.
  2. Open the email draft and attach that downloaded image yourself.
  3. Review the message and send it. If no email app opens, copy the submission text and email it with the attachment to trymanymaybes@gmail.com.

Selecting a file or preparing it does not send it. Submissions must be sent by someone aged 18 or older. A parent or guardian may send a younger creator’s work with permission, without the child’s real name or contact details.

5. What happens after sending?

We review submissions manually. Nothing appears automatically, and sending does not guarantee publication. If selected, we will reply with a gallery link. Your sender email will not appear in the gallery.

Individual gallery pages provide a link and an image to share. Embed code is for websites and blogs; social apps generally use the page link or uploaded image instead. Comments are not open during this pilot.

Only share work you own or have permission to share. Tell us if you used code or AI assistance so we can describe the process accurately. See Privacy & participation for publication and removal details.
