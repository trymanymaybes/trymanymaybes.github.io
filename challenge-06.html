Challenge 06 · Suggest material through visual evidence

What is this made of?

Three wavy lines. Can the same marks feel soft, flowing or solid?

Your challenge

  1. Keep all three original wavy lines visible and in their original relative positions.
  2. Add around or between the lines; do not erase or cover any original stroke.
  3. Rotate or uniformly resize the whole starter only. Do not move individual lines.
  4. Imagine three different materials. Develop one using at least two visual clues.
  5. Do not write the material name inside the picture. Let the added clues suggest it.

Beginner route: list three materials, choose one, then add just two clues. Simple lines count.

Try before you peek

Pause and imagine your own materials before opening the examples. There is no single correct answer.

Optional hints
  1. Could these lines be folds, currents, grain or something else?
  2. What edge, surface detail or response to movement would support your idea?
  3. Use two different kinds of evidence, rather than relying only on colour.

Reflect

Which clue changed the way the lines felt? Remove that clue mentally: what else could the image become?

Why this challenge?

After context, negative space, scale, time and relationships, explore how visual evidence suggests material. This is creative practice, not a test or a promise of therapeutic benefit.
