Challenge 08 · Unexpected function

What else could it do?

Keep the spoon outline intact. Give it an unexpected job and add a setting that shows how it works; its new purpose must be something other than eating or serving food.

Your challenge

  1. Keep the spoon outline intact. Give it an unexpected job and add a setting that shows how it works; its new purpose must be something other than eating or serving food.
  2. Keep every original mark visible and preserve its position relative to the other marks. Do not erase, cover or move individual marks.
  3. You may rotate or uniformly resize the whole starter.
  4. Try three ideas before choosing one. Add at least two clues that make your interpretation understandable.

Rough sketches are welcome. You can also imagine the scene or describe your interpretation in words.
