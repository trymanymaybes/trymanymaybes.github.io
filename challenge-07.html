Challenge 07 · Observer perspective

Whose eyes are these?

Keep the rectangle and two short parallel lines. Imagine the scene through an unusual observer’s eyes, then add clues that show where the observer is and what they can see.

Your challenge

  1. Keep the rectangle and two short parallel lines. Imagine the scene through an unusual observer’s eyes, then add clues that show where the observer is and what they can see.
  2. Keep every original mark visible and preserve its position relative to the other marks. Do not erase, cover or move individual marks.
  3. You may rotate or uniformly resize the whole starter.
  4. Try three ideas before choosing one. Add at least two clues that make your interpretation understandable.

Rough sketches are welcome. You can also imagine the scene or describe your interpretation in words.
