Challenge 10 · Opposite interpretations

Can both be true?

Use the same circle, rectangle and short line in two separate pictures. Add different surroundings so the two versions support opposite readings, such as welcome and unwelcome, or help and obstacle.

Your challenge

  1. Use the same circle, rectangle and short line in two separate pictures. Add different surroundings so the two versions support opposite readings, such as welcome and unwelcome, or help and obstacle.
  2. Keep every original mark visible and preserve its position relative to the other marks. Do not erase, cover or move individual marks.
  3. You may rotate or uniformly resize the whole starter.
  4. Make and save the first interpretation, then start the same challenge again for the second. Keep the starter identical in both versions.

Rough sketches are welcome. You can also imagine the scene or describe your interpretation in words.
