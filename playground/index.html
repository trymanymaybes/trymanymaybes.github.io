Add an example to the end of your script, then click Run:
Python 3-style syntax via Skulpt: loops, functions, lists and classes. Imports: turtle, math, random. This is not full desktop Python.
Movement: forward / fd, backward / bk, left / lt, right / rt, goto, home, setheading, circle. Pen: penup, pendown, pensize, pencolor, fillcolor, color, begin_fill, end_fill, dot, write. Supports Turtle(), Screen() and module-level calls. Coordinates: (0, 0) is the centre, positive Y points up.
The completed result appears after Run. speed(), hideturtle(), setup(), tracer() and done() are compatibility no-ops. Mouse/key callbacks, Tk windows, external packages, files and network access are unavailable. Font family, bold and italic are supported when available on your device. Background colour stays beneath the starter. setup() does not resize the challenge canvas. Limits: 64 KB script, 12,000 drawing commands, 100,000 turtle points, approximately 4 seconds of Python execution; an independent timeout stops the worker.
