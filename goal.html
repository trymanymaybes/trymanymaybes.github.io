Our goal

Make room for another possibility.

We want to make creative exploration easy to begin, interesting to continue and rewarding to share.

For the person taking part

Our aim is deliberate creative practice: noticing a first interpretation, changing an assumption, exploring alternatives and reflecting on what changed. We curate constraints around these thinking moves. We also value a small moment of discovery: trying an idea you would not usually try, enjoying an unexpected result, or realising that another person saw something completely different.

You can spend a few minutes or stay with a challenge longer. Completing the activity privately is just as valid as sharing it.

What we are learning with Challenge 01

This is our first public pilot. We want to learn whether the instructions are clear, whether the constraints invite interesting interpretations, and whether seeing other approaches makes people curious to keep exploring.

We are not presenting this activity as a test of intelligence or artistic ability, or promising measurable improvements in creativity.

Where it could grow

We hope to explore more starting points: unfinished illustrations, everyday objects, abstract patterns and open-ended creative problems. Future collections might work on paper, on screen or in a shared workshop. We will develop them in response to what participants find worthwhile.

For now, there is one challenge. We have no fixed release schedule.

Help shape the next step

After trying it, tell us what felt inviting, what was confusing and what surprised you. Feedback is welcome even if you choose not to share your work.
