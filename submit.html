Challenge 01 · Your interpretation

Show us your maybe.

Prepare a copy here, then send it by email. We review submissions before anything appears in the gallery.

Selecting a file does not upload it. This page works locally in your browser. Sending is a separate step in your email app; your sender address will be visible to Many Maybes, but won't be included in the gallery.

Choose a photo of the work only. Avoid faces, addresses, signatures or other details you don't want published. Maximum 24 megapixels.

For this pilot, submissions must be sent by someone aged 18 or older. A parent or guardian may send a younger creator's work with their permission, without the child's real name or contact details.

Your prepared copy

The prepared JPEG is resized and re-encoded without the original file metadata. Check the visible image for personal details before sending.

  1. Download the prepared image.
  2. Open the email draft and attach that downloaded image.
  3. Review and send from your email app.
2. Open email draft

Email app unavailable? Send the prepared image and copied text to trymanymaybes@gmail.com. The draft link cannot attach the image automatically.

Preparing a file or opening a draft does not mean it was sent or accepted. If selected, we will reply with the gallery link. You can request removal by replying to that email.
