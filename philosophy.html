Our philosophy

There is more than one way to see it.

The same beginning can lead to radically different ideas. That difference is the point.

A starting point, not an answer

A circle might suggest a wheel, a world, a pause or something without a name. Many Maybes invites you to notice your first interpretation, then ask: what else could this become?

Constraints make the invitation interesting

Each challenge gives you a small set of rules. They create a shared starting point and something to work with. Within those boundaries, the direction is yours. We are not hiding a correct solution.

Ideas before polish

You do not need to draw beautifully to see something unexpected. A rough sketch, a few words or an unfinished experiment can communicate an interesting idea. Experienced creators are welcome too; technical skill is one way to express an interpretation, not the measure of its worth.

Different perspectives deserve room

The gallery is an invitation to understand how someone else saw the same beginning. We value curiosity, experimentation and the story behind an idea. There are no rankings or scores in this pilot.

Tools are optional

Paper and a pencil are enough for the first challenge. Digital tools, mathematics and code offer other ways to explore. We are also curious about human and AI-assisted interpretations, without assuming that one approach is inherently more creative. Those comparisons are a future exploration, not a requirement for taking part.

Try Challenge 01 ↗

Practise a thinking move

Changing viewpoint, scale, time, function or material can open another interpretation. A useful constraint gives you a reason to try one of these moves. Details help an imagined world become coherent; polish is optional. Ask what you assumed, what you changed and what you noticed.
