Privacy & participation

Updated 13 October 2026.

Using the website

You can read the challenge and browse the examples without signing in or submitting work. Public browsing adds no analytics or advertising. Preparing an image stays local in your browser. Optional direct submission is being configured and remains disabled. GitHub hosts the site and may process request information such as your IP address under its own privacy statement.

GitHub's privacy statement

Preparing an image

The preparation tool reads the file you select locally in your browser. It creates a resized JPEG without copying the original file metadata. It does not upload your file. Your draft is not saved across page reloads. The visible contents of the image can still identify you: remove personal information before sending.

Sending a submission

Submissions go by email to trymanymaybes@gmail.com. Your email provider and Gmail process that email. Many Maybes receives your sender address, attachment and message so it can review the work and reply. Use a public pseudonym if you prefer. Do not send passwords, identity documents, phone numbers, addresses or other sensitive details.

Optional direct submission

Direct submission is currently disabled. When enabled, Google and Supabase will handle sign-in, and Cloudflare will check requests for automated abuse. Your signed-in session and submission receipt will be held in this browser tab. Your Google name, email and profile photo will not be used as public gallery credits.

Direct uploads will be held privately for review, together with the title, pseudonym and explanation you choose. An IP hash will support submission limits. Nothing will appear publicly without review. Retention and deletion arrangements for direct uploads will be finalized before this option opens.

Publication and moderation

Nothing is published automatically. Send only work you own or are authorized to share. Gallery publication requires your permission. Public fields are the reviewed image, title, chosen pseudonym and explanation. Sender addresses, original email headers and private correspondence are not gallery content.

For this pilot, the sender must be 18 or older. A parent or guardian may submit a younger creator's work with permission, without identifying the child. We may decline content that contains personal information, abusive material, third-party work without permission, or an image that does not fit the challenge. Selection is not a ranking of artistic skill.

Removal and email retention

Reply to your submission email to withdraw permission or request deletion of the submission email and removal from the gallery. This is a manual process; there is no automated deletion schedule. We can remove content we control, but cannot guarantee removal from downloaded copies, search caches or other people's shares. Email-provider backups may follow separate retention rules.

Sharing and comments

Published pages have shareable links and image downloads. Website embeds and social previews depend on the receiving platform. Public comments and popularity rankings are not enabled during the pilot.

Contact

For questions, privacy requests or a security concern, email trymanymaybes@gmail.com. Do not include sensitive details in a public repository issue.
