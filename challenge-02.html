Challenge 02 · Reinterpret negative space

The gap does the work

Keep the two filled shapes in place and leave the central gap completely blank. Add clues on the shapes or outside the gap so the untouched space becomes the most important part of your picture.

Your challenge

  1. Keep both original shapes, their filled silhouettes and their relative positions.
  2. Leave the central gap completely blank: no drawing, colour, words or pasted objects inside it.
  3. Add details on the shapes and outside the gap. You may rotate or uniformly resize the whole starter.
  4. First jot down three different roles for the gap; develop one. Artistic polish is optional.
Draw this challenge ↗

Use freehand tools or turtle code in your browser. No installation or account needed. Keep the gap blank as you draw.
