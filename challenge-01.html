Challenge 01

These four marks are your starting point.

Paper or digital · No account needed

A few marks,
many possibilities.

Turn these marks into an unexpected scene, object or idea.

  1. Keep every original mark visible, with its position relative to the other marks unchanged.
  2. Add freely: lines, shapes, words or colour.
  3. Try three meanings: an object, a place and something happening. Give your favourite a title and describe the assumption you changed.

You may rotate or uniformly scale the whole starter. Don't move, erase or rotate individual marks. Rough sketches are welcome; there's no single correct answer.
